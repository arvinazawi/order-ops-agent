# Order Operations Policy (sample)

## Returns
Audience: all
Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. A return is started from the order page or by contacting support with the order number. Final-sale and custom-made items cannot be returned.
Customers often ask: can I send it back?

## Refunds
Audience: all
Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the warehouse receives and inspects the item. Shipping fees are refunded only when the return is caused by our error. Orders delivered more than 5 business days after the promised date qualify for a shipping fee refund on request.

## Shipping delays
Audience: all
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. If a carrier marks an order as delayed, support opens a carrier trace after 2 business days. Customers are emailed whenever the estimated delivery date changes.

## Order cancellation
Audience: all
Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they reach picking status, which usually happens within 1 hour of purchase. After that, the customer must wait for delivery and then start a return.

## Damaged or wrong items
Audience: all
Damaged or wrong items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with a photo. We send a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund including shipping. The customer does not need to send the damaged item back unless support asks for it.

## Goodwill credits and employee discounts
Audience: hr
Support agents may issue goodwill credits up to 20 dollars without approval; anything above 20 dollars needs a team lead. Employees receive a 25 percent discount on up to 10 orders a year. This section is internal and must never be shown to customers or sales reps.
